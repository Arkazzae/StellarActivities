# Whiteboard artwork

The cover and the marker icon were created for this project using the built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool, then scaled to 1536 × 864 (WebP) and 512 × 512 (PNG).

Icon prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Square 1:1 finished app icon for "Whiteboard", a shared drawing board where friends sketch diagrams together during a voice call. Design one bold custom mark: a stout marker pen, seen at a slight diagonal, finishing a single loose hand-drawn loop that becomes an arrow, drawn in warm cream ink on the field. Add one small tomato-red dot as an accent where the stroke begins. Deep cobalt ultramarine field all the way to the edges of the square. A graphic designer's two-to-three-ink screen-printed identity mark: flat ink, precise cut-paper silhouette for the pen, the drawn stroke clearly hand-made with slight irregular edges. Oversized centered mark with roughly 16% breathing room, recognizable at 24 px. No text, letters, initials, extra marks, drop shadows, gradients, gloss, 3D, or rounded corners on the canvas. Only the square icon, viewed straight on, full bleed.

Cover prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Wide 16:9 flat screen-printed editorial illustration for "Whiteboard", a collaborative sketch board inside a voice chat app. Subject: SKETCHING TOGETHER. In the right two thirds, a big warm-cream board floats at a slight angle; on it, loose hand-drawn diagrams: three rounded boxes connected by wobbly arrows, a sticky note, a quick doodle of a rocket, a dashed loop, and two or three tiny cursor arrows each trailing its own stroke, as if several people are drawing at once. Two or three stylised hands holding chunky markers reach into the board from the edges. Inks: deep cobalt ultramarine, warm cream, near-black ink and one tomato-red spot accent; cut-paper and screenprint poster look with selective halftone and precise irregular ink edges, contemporary print studio rather than stock vector. Leave the left third as rich, mostly empty cobalt for the in-app title. Flat 2D edge to edge, no photographic or 3D rendering, no readable words or letters anywhere (diagram labels are abstract squiggles), no logos, no watermark, no neon.
