# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Groups of friends playing together during a voice or video call. One person
hosts a room and the rest join as guests, usually for a short, social session
where the rules must be understandable without a tutorial.

## Product Purpose

Doodle Drift turns short sentences into shared drawing-and-caption chains. The
product succeeds when players can join quickly, understand what to do in every
round, draw comfortably on their current device and enjoy the final reveal
together. The current development slice supports one complete classic mode and
uses a local mock activity adapter instead of the production SDK.

## Brand Personality

Playful, tactile and clear. The tone is friendly and lightly absurd without
becoming childish. The paper, crayon and observatory world should make each
chain feel like a small object the group created together.

## Anti-references

- A visual or interaction clone of Gartic Phone.
- Neon casino interfaces, streamer overlays and competitive score-heavy games.
- Corporate dashboards or generic component-library screens.
- Platform-dependent emoji or mismatched stock illustrations used as the main identity.
- Decorative effects that obscure the prompt, canvas or next required action.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the next action obvious at every point in the chain.
2. Give the canvas priority over surrounding controls.
3. Let personality come from authored assets and small shared reactions.
4. Keep controls familiar enough to use without instruction.
5. Treat mobile and compact activity frames as first-class play surfaces.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast. All drawing controls need visible focus states,
keyboard-accessible actions and text labels for assistive technology. State
must not depend on color alone. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep the
core flow usable without decorative animation.
