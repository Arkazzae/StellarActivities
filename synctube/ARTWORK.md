# SyncTube artwork

The watch-party cover and playback icon were created for this project using the built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool.

Cover prompt
Use case: illustration-story. Wide 16:9 flat screen-printed editorial illustration for SyncTube, a YouTube watch party. Make WATCHING TOGETHER the subject: five distinct friends occupy the lower right half, graphic head-and-shoulder silhouettes from behind or in three-quarter profile, one curly, one in a cap, one with a bun, clustered naturally on one long sofa watching the same enormous floating YouTube-style video player. Their faces are turned to it. On that player: one boldly oversized vermilion red rounded play button with a white triangle in an empty warm-cream frame, and a single red progress bar with its round scrubber. The recognizable player and five people are the whole story. A cut-paper and screenprint poster in three spot inks, tomato red, warm cream and deep near-black plum, with precise irregular ink edges and selective halftone. Strong silhouette, contemporary print studio rather than stock vector. On the left third leave rich unprinted dark plum for the actual in-app title, weight people/player to right; the upper right and lower right should both reward attention. Flat 2D from edge to edge, poster composition, expressive abstract shapes rather than any photographic or rendered models. No movement/sports, no skateboards, no depicted video content, no cinema seats, no large lettering, no captions, no watermark, no neon. Image only.

Cover revision prompt
Precise edit to the provided accepted SyncTube illustrated cover: replace ONLY the huge red YouTube play-logo in the upper right cream player picture area with visibly PLAYABLE original 2D gameplay. Inside the existing monitor, show a bold side-scrolling pixel-art platformer frame: a small unmistakable cream explorer sprite mid-jump from a floating block platform to the next over a gap, raised red brick steps, a few collectible diamond shapes in a guiding arc and distant pixel landscape. Keep the game clear, sharp and composed like a screenshot rather than a game poster, using only plum, cream and tomato red to harmonize with the surrounding screenprint. No words, numbers, recognizable game characters or giant play button within the game. Preserve precisely the scale, placement and silhouettes of the five friends watching from their sofa, their hairstyles, the existing video player's outer outline and controls including its slim red progress bar, dark left-hand space for copy, margins, screenprint halftones, landscape 16:9 canvas and paper grain. All other elements stay the same.

Icon prompt
Use case: logo-brand. Square 1:1 finished app icon for SyncTube, a synchronized video watch party. Design a single custom teletext-era video mark: two deliberately offset broadcast frames in solid warm ivory, stacked at a slight diagonal, the foreground frame a stout horizontal screen with a single right-facing play triangle cut out of its solid face. Back frame peeks clearly along the upper left edge. A crisp, iconic and unusual cut-paper silhouette. Screen-print vermilion red field all the way to the edges of the square. A graphic designer's two-ink 1970s public-television ident; physically printed flat ink, refined hand-cut geometry, no sketchiness. Strong black-and-white relationship, oversized centered mark with 16% breathing room, recognizable at 24px. No text, initials, extra marks, shadows, gradients, gloss, corner rounding on the icon canvas or exact YouTube logo. Only the square icon, viewed straight on full bleed.

The bundled sample clip uses an earlier cinema illustration created for SyncTube and original synthesized audio made from 110 Hz and 164.81 Hz sine waves.
