# SyncTube changelog

## Unreleased

- Reduced the installable archive to player assets and their licenses. Artwork production notes are available in the source repository.

## 1.1.0

- Open a private queue from **Friends → Activities → Launch for myself**, including when you are outside a voice call.
- Continue to use the shared queue from a voice channel. Playback and queued videos belong to the session you opened.

## 1.0.1

- Added a screen-printed cover of five friends watching a platform game and a new playback icon. Both appear in the Stellarr activity library.

## 1.0.0

- Added synchronized YouTube playback, a shared queue, seeking, skipping, and a bundled sample clip.
- Added per-listener volume and host transfer. Late joiners catch up to the room’s current playback position.
