# SyncTube changelog

## 0.2.0

- Start shared watch parties in voice channels. SyncTube has been removed from the solo activity picker.

- New layout with Queue, History, People and Settings tabs beside the player. Paste links at the top of the queue to see the title and start time before adding.
- Vote to skip. More than half of the people online skips the video. The host and the person who started the room can skip without a vote, and everyone sees who did. The instance owner can too once Stellarr marks owners in the participant list.
- The host chooses whether everyone or only the host controls playback, and turns autoplay and repeat on or off. Guests can still add videos and remove their own.
- History lists watched and skipped videos so anyone can queue them again.
- Copy a link at the current time, open the video on YouTube, switch to cinema mode, resync from the sync indicator and use keyboard shortcuts.
- Shared playback speed on dedicated activity hosts and in local previews.
- Polish and English. SyncTube follows Stellarr’s language, and **Settings → Language** overrides it.
- Your spot in each video and your language choice are saved to your account. Empty rooms offer to start where you left off.
- Avatars and usernames from Stellarr appear in the People tab.
- The room fits the activity panel at every size, from a phone to full screen. The player stays the largest 16:9 picture that fits, the tabs sit beside it or under it, and only the lists scroll. Room details moved to **Settings → The room**, and errors show at the top.

## 0.1.1

- Play YouTube videos through Stellarr’s player when the activity shares the instance hostname.

## 0.1.0

- Watch YouTube together in a voice channel. Everyone can add videos to the queue, seek and skip. Late joiners catch up to the current playback position.
- Open a private queue from **Friends → Activities → Launch for myself**, including outside a voice call.
- Set your listening volume independently. Transfer playback control to another host.
- Try the bundled sample clip offline. The activity library displays a playback icon and an illustrated watch-party cover.
